About the real test

  • 50 multiple-choice questions covering the general knowledge chapters of the Oregon CDL manual.
  • 80% to pass — you can miss at most 10 questions.
  • 1 hour to complete it at the DMV.
  • Class A applicants also need the Air Brakes and Combination Vehicles knowledge tests. Those topics are included in this study guide.

How to use this site

  1. Read through the Study Guide — focus on the "Key numbers to memorize" callouts.
  2. Take the Practice Test. Answers are explained as you go.
  3. Re-take the test a few times — the question order is reshuffled each session.

Official sources

The questions on this site are paraphrased from the Oregon DMV Commercial Driver Manual, which is what the state test is written from. Always confirm current rules with the official manual before your test.
